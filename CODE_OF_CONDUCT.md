# Code of Conduct

Be decent. Discuss proofs and wording in good faith. Disagreement about design is welcome;
personal attacks are not.

Unacceptable behavior gets a warning; repeated or egregious behavior gets blocked from the
repository. Maintainer discretion applies.

Honest citation is the conduct that matters most here: do not claim a result is certified
under a standard it has not been certified under, and do not relicense fenced copies as
[CFSF](LICENSE).
